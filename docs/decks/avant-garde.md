# The Avant-Garde Domain

The highest tier of esoteric plugins. The Avant-Garde collection spans macro-astrophysics, micro-biology, advanced cryptography, and dark financial arbitrage.

---

## TurboPulsar (Astronomy)
Astrophysical metronome emulating extreme stellar bodies.

* **Simulation:** Emulates the precise rotational period of neutron stars (Pulsars) by emitting compressed white-noise radiation bursts. 
* **Filter:** Includes an interstellar Dispersion Measure (DM) filter which smears lower frequencies based on intergalactic distance plasma.

---

## TurboGenome (Biology)
Biological sequence arpeggiator. 

* **The Code:** Crawls through literal DNA nucleotide strings (Adenine, Cytosine, Guanine, Thymine) via a high-speed stochastic loop.
* **Transcription:** Converts genetic bounds into mathematically scaled acidic synth arpeggios, featuring real-time artificial mutation rates to break deterministic sequences.

## TurboMorse (Cryptography)
Cryptography war transmitter. 

* **The Translation:** Takes arbitrary standard text strings and encodes them into military-grade Telegraph signals (Dots and Dashes).
* **The Output:** The signal is immediately routed through an overcranked, hard-clipping WaveShaper node, ideal for injecting raw, distorted subliminal codes into a heavy industrial mix.

## TurboFractal (Mathematics)
Non-linear mathematical drone. 

* **The Equation:** Navigates the complex plane of the Mandelbrot set ($Z_{n+1} = Z_n^2 + C$). 
* **The Timbre:** Maps the fractal's escape velocity directly onto a custom `PeriodicWave` partial-harmonic spectrum. We reshape the physical overtone series of the drone oscillator in real-time based purely on fractal geometry.
