# The Absurd Collection

The Absurd Series experiments with non-linear control mechanisms, turning chaotic mathematics and live environmental data into driving musical forces.

---

## TurboBrain
Binaural beats generator for psychoacoustic entrainment.

* **Psychoacoustics:** By panning pure sine tones slightly offset left and right (e.g. 400Hz vs 410Hz), the human brain hallucinates a third 10Hz phantom frequency.
* **Mapping:** Presets built specifically for Gamma (Focus), Beta, Alpha, Theta, and Delta (Deep Sleep) entrainment stages.

---

## TurboGeiger
Hardware-free Geiger-Müller counter simulation.

* **Mathematics:** Mathematical Poisson processes emulate true radioactive isotope decay.
* **Application:** Provides absolutely un-quantized, stochastic drum machine transients. There is no grid; only probability and decay.

---

## TurboWeather
Real-time environmental atmospheric synthesizer.

* **Sourcing:** Uses the `Open-Meteo` API to grab live data of the exact geographical coordinates of the user.
* **Audio-Mapping:** Temperature shifts alter pink noise filters. Global wind speed drives LFO amplitude. Rain forecasts synthesize randomized percussive droplet generators. 

---

## TurboBoid
Artificial Life / Flocking Algorithm Sequencer.

* **Simulation:** Implements classic Boid algorithms (Separation, Alignment, Cohesion) on an abstract 2D plane.
* **Triggering:** If a simulated node crosses the boundaries of the field, it triggers generative geometric audio spikes. The music literally controls itself via swarm intelligence.

---

## TurboSonar
Deep-water ping sonar mapping.

* **Synthesis:** Impulses high-frequency oscillator pings into a synthetic abyssal `ConvolverNode`.
* **Timbre:** The reverberation is an artificial 5-second long-tail decay designed to simulate immense submarine pressure networks.
